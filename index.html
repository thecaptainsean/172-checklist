About this checklist

A free, interactive checklist built for the 1973 Cessna 172M Skyhawk, derived from the original Pilot Operating Handbook. It runs entirely in your browser — nothing to install, no account, no fees.

  • 31 sections covering preflight through securing the aircraft, plus 13 emergency procedures
  • Works fully offline once loaded — reliable in the cockpit with no signal
  • Color-coded category bars (preflight, engine, departure, in-flight, emergency)
  • Light, dark, and red night-vision display modes
  • Five color palettes, four fonts, adjustable text size for cockpit readability
  • Tap to check items — sections turn green when complete
  • Full edit mode — rename anything, add or remove items, customize to your aircraft
  • Saves your tail number, edits, and preferences locally on your device

About the pilot

Built by Sean Bates, a private pilot based in St. Petersburg, Florida, flying a 1973 Cessna 172M out of the Tampa Bay area.

Bates Aviation is a personal project to share free pilot tools with the general aviation community. If you find this useful, share it with another 172 pilot.
